# side groups

Generates a list of sideGroups from a logical expression of adjacent sides. Used with bordersSides.

```sig
tileScanner.sideGroups(CollisionDirection.Left)
```

## Parameters

* **side1**: A CollisionDirection
* **op1**: A tileScanner.LogicOp
* **side2**: A CollisionDirection
* **op2**: A tileScanner.LogicOp
* **side3**: A CollisionDirection
* **op3**: A tileScanner.LogicOp
* **side4**: A CollisionDirection
* **op4**: A tileScanner.LogicOp
* **side5**: A CollisionDirection
* **op5**: A tileScanner.LogicOp
* **side6**: A CollisionDirection
* **op6**: A tileScanner.LogicOp
* **side7**: A CollisionDirection
* **op7**: A tileScanner.LogicOp
* **side8**: A CollisionDirection
* **op8**: A tileScanner.LogicOp
* **side9**: A CollisionDirection
* **op9**: A tileScanner.LogicOp
* **side10**: A CollisionDirection
* **op10**: A tileScanner.LogicOp
* **side11**: A CollisionDirection
* **op11**: A tileScanner.LogicOp
* **side12**: A CollisionDirection
